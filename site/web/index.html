Say hi to Mnemonic

A 126M parameter model, trained from scratch on one RTX 5070 Ti. The tokenizer, the training, and the engine running it right now in this tab are all hand-written assembly: x86, PTX for the GPU, and WebAssembly here.

It runs on your own CPU, so nothing you type leaves your computer. It's small, so expect confident mistakes.
